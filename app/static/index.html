YouTube Video Summarizer

Drop a YouTube link. Get a clean summary and full transcript — no sign-up, no fluff, just the takeaways.

01 — Download
Any public YouTube video

Paste any URL — lectures, podcasts, interviews, tutorials. yt-dlp extracts the audio stream directly, no browser needed.

02 — Transcribe
Word-accurate speech-to-text

OpenAI Whisper runs locally against the audio track, producing a clean verbatim transcript regardless of accent or background noise.

03 — Summarise
Dense, readable summary

The transcript is sent to a Groq-hosted LLM. You get back a tight, structured summary you can skim in 30 seconds.
